About VoltTest

For over 14 years, VoltTest has engineered the battery testing equipment that laboratories, manufacturers and EV makers rely on to prove their batteries safe, consistent and durable.

Our Story

Born in the Lab. Built for the Factory Floor.

VoltTest was founded in 2012 by a team of power electronics and electrochemistry engineers who believed that battery quality should never be a guess. What began as a laboratory cycler project is now a full-line battery test system supplier serving 40+ countries.

Today, our battery test systems power R&D laboratories, gigafactory formation lines and EV pack production plants. We remain a hands-on engineering company: every system is designed, calibrated and factory-accepted by the people who built it.

  • ✓ 600+ systems installed worldwide
  • ✓ In-house R&D, manufacturing and software teams
  • ✓ OEM/ODM battery test solutions under full NDA
  • ✓ Global service network with remote diagnostics
