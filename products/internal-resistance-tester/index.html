Battery Internal Resistance Testers

Measure cell health in milliseconds. ACIR and DCIR instruments for battery production QC, cell matching and formation grading.

IR Tester

Fast, Accurate Resistance Measurement on the Production Line

The VT-IR series is the battery testing equipment of choice for internal resistance verification. With both 1 kHz ACIR and high-current DCIR modes, it reliably detects welding defects, electrolyte problems and cell degradation before batteries leave your plant.

  • ✓ ACIR 1 kHz measurement, 0.1 µΩ to 3 kΩ range
  • ✓ DCIR pulse measurement up to 300 A for module-level testing
  • ✓ ±0.5% + 3 digit basic accuracy
  • ✓ 10 ms measurement speed, suitable for inline automation
  • ✓ Four-wire Kelvin probes, sorting I/O and barcode scanning
