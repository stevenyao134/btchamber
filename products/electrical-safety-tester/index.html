Electrical Safety Test Equipment

Hi-pot, insulation resistance and ground continuity testers — the final electrical safety gate for batteries, chargers, inverters and power equipment.

Electrical Safety

Verify Insulation. Prevent the Shocking Failure.

The VT-ESA series performs the full suite of electrical safety tests required by IEC 61010, UL and GB 4706: AC/DC hi-pot, insulation resistance and ground continuity — with pass/fail decisions in seconds.

  • ✓ AC hi-pot to 5 kV, DC hi-pot to 6 kV, 10 mA leakage
  • ✓ Insulation resistance to 1 TΩ at up to 5 kV test voltage
  • ✓ Ground continuity: 30 A, 0.001–0.5 Ω range
  • ✓ Ramp, dwell and discharge control per test standards
  • ✓ Remote PLC I/O, RS-232 and Ethernet integration
