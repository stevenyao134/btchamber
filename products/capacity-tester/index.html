Battery Capacity Testers

Verify true battery capacity with coulomb-counting precision — from single-cell laboratory checks to 512-channel production grading.

Capacity Testing

Know Exactly What Your Cells Deliver

The VT-CT series measures discharge capacity, charge capacity, energy throughput and coulombic efficiency with 0.05% current accuracy. Combined with the VoltTest grading software, it automatically sorts cells into capacity bins for consistent battery pack performance.

  • ✓ CC, CP and CR discharge modes for full characterisation
  • ✓ Up to 512 channels in one cabinet for production grading
  • ✓ 0.05% FS accuracy, 1 ms data acquisition
  • ✓ Automatic binning, sorting and label printing
  • ✓ Rechargeable regenerative discharge ≥90% efficiency
