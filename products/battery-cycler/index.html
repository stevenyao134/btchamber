Battery Charge / Discharge Test Systems

Professional battery cyclers for R&D, QC and production — accurate, repeatable and built for continuous 24/7 operation.

Battery Cycler

Precision Charge/Discharge Testing for Every Cell Chemistry

The VT-CDS series is VoltTest's flagship battery cycler family. Each channel runs independently with CC, CV, CP and CR modes, making it the ideal battery testing equipment for cycle life studies, rate capability tests, calendar aging and battery modelling.

  • ✓ Independent channels: 8 / 16 / 32 per 19-inch rack
  • ✓ Voltage ranges 0–5 V, 0–10 V, 0–60 V, 0–200 V
  • ✓ Current from 1 mA to 300 A per channel
  • ✓ ±0.05% FS accuracy, 1 ms data logging
  • ✓ Regenerative discharge: ≥90% energy recovery

Key Features

Engineered for the Battery Laboratory

◎

High Accuracy

16-bit ADC, four-wire Kelvin sensing and calibrated references deliver ±0.05% FS voltage and current accuracy.

⚡

Fast Sampling

1 ms data logging captures voltage, current, power and internal resistance dynamics during pulse testing.

↺

Energy Recovery

Regenerative discharge feeds ≥90% of energy back to the grid, reducing heat and operating cost.

▤

VoltTest Studio

Graphical test programming, cycle statistics, database export, and MES/ERP integration for traceable data.

⊞

Multi-Channel

Up to 32 independent channels per rack, expandable to hundreds of channels in one test bench.

🔒

Safety Interlocks

Over-voltage, over-current, over-temperature protection and alarm chain per channel.

⇄

BMS Interfaces

CAN, RS-485 and Ethernet communication for battery management system testing.

⌛

24/7 Duty

Industrial-grade power stages with forced-air cooling designed for continuous production use.
