EV Battery Test Solutions

A complete testing chain for electric vehicle batteries — from cell development to pack end-of-line — engineered for automotive-grade reliability and traceability.

The Challenge

EV Batteries Demand End-to-End Test Integrity

An EV battery must deliver thousands of safe cycles across extreme temperatures. Every cell, module and pack needs verification — but test lines must also keep pace with fast production takt times. VoltTest designs test solutions that balance precision, speed and cost across the whole chain.

  • ✓ Cell-level: cycling, DCIR, formation, safety and 100% inline verification
  • ✓ Module-level: high-power cycling, BMS CAN testing, thermal cycling
  • ✓ Pack-level: EOL testing, insulation & hi-pot, drive-cycle simulation
  • ✓ Data: complete traceability from electrode batch to finished pack

Solution Workflow

A Typical EV Battery Test Flow

1

Cell Formation

VT-FG systems form and grade cells with energy recovery and MES data capture.

2

Cell QC

VT-IR testers verify internal resistance, OCV and sorting before module assembly.

3

Module & Pack Test

VT-MP benches cycle modules and packs with BMS communication and thermal control.

4

EOL & Shipping

VT-ESA electrical safety tests plus final EOL sequence complete the line.
