Battery Pack End-of-Line Testing

The final quality gate before a pack ships. Automated EOL test benches that verify electrical safety, performance and BMS behaviour — in minutes, with full traceability.

The Challenge

Every Pack Must Pass the Gate — Fast

End-of-line testing is the last chance to catch defects before a battery pack reaches a vehicle or grid. It must be comprehensive enough to guarantee safety, yet fast enough to meet production takt time. VoltTest builds EOL benches that run the complete verification sequence automatically.

  • ✓ Insulation resistance and AC/DC hi-pot testing per GB 38031 / IEC 62660
  • ✓ OCV and cell-group DCIR measurement with balancing check
  • ✓ BMS wake-up, CAN communication and SOC algorithm validation
  • ✓ Short charge/discharge EOL cycle with temperature monitoring
  • ✓ Pass/fail gating with barcode-linked records in your MES

Solution Workflow

Typical EOL Test Sequence

1

Insulation & Hi-Pot

Verify electrical isolation of the pack against chassis per safety standards.

2

OCV / DCIR

Measure open-circuit voltage and resistance of every cell group; check balance.

3

BMS & Comms

Wake the BMS, validate CAN communication, SOC estimation and fault detection.

4

EOL Cycle

Short charge/discharge cycle with temperature checks, then pass/fail release.
